# Third-party notices

UIButton's variant structure and utility styles are adapted from [shadcn-vue](https://github.com/unovue/shadcn-vue), including its [Button](https://github.com/unovue/shadcn-vue/tree/dev/apps/v4/registry/new-york-v4/ui/button). The component uses a native button instead of the upstream polymorphic primitive, with project-specific sizes and color tokens.

UIInput's base utility styles follow shadcn-vue's Input, with native Vue model binding instead of VueUse and project-specific sizes, states, and tokens.
